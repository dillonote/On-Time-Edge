NOTES FOR BDRs

  • Every email has ONE ask. Don't add links, attachments, or calendly
    unless your sequence tool requires it.
  • Emails 1–5 establish the problem. Don't pitch OTE yet.
  • Emails 6–7 are proof. Pick the case study closest to the prospect's
    industry.
  • Emails 8–9 handle the two biggest objections preemptively.
  • Emails 10–12 reframe for executives and explain the engagement model.
  • Emails 13–15 are trigger-based — swap in whichever matches the
    prospect's pain (OTIF, overtime, growth).
  • Emails 16–18 are the wind-down. Don't add more after the breakup.
  • Mix calls between emails 4/5, 9/10, and 15/16.
  • Add LinkedIn connection request around Day 7.
